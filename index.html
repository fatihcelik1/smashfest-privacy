Privacy Policy

App: Smash Fest!
Developer: FCgames
Last updated: 26 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile game Smash Fest! ("the App", "we", "us") handles information when you use it. By using the App you agree to the practices described below.

1. Information We Collect

We do not ask you to create an account and we do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information (such as your name, email or phone number) directly through the App.

The App uses third-party services that may collect information used to identify your device and understand app usage. This may include:

  • Device advertising identifier (Advertising ID)
  • Approximate, non-precise device and usage information (device model, OS version, app interactions, crash data)
  • App usage / event data (for example levels started and completed, buttons and features used)
  • IP address (used by ad and analytics services)

Game progress and settings (such as your level, coins and boosters) are stored only on your device.

2. Advertising

The App displays advertisements through Google AdMob. AdMob may use the device Advertising ID and related information to show personalized or non-personalized ads and to measure ad performance. You can reset or limit ad personalization in your device settings ("Ads" / "Reset advertising ID").

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, the App asks for your consent the first time it runs, using Google's User Messaging Platform. You choose there whether ads may be personalized, and you can change that choice at any time from the App's privacy options. Declining does not remove ads; it makes them non-personalized.

For more information about how Google uses data, see Google's Privacy & Terms.

3. Analytics

The App uses Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how players use the game (for example which levels are played, completed or failed) so we can improve it. This may include device information, app usage/event data and an app-instance identifier. See Firebase Privacy and Security for details.

4. Notifications

The App can send local reminder notifications (for example when lives have refilled) to encourage you to come back. These notifications are scheduled on your device and do not transmit personal information to us. You can turn them off inside the App's settings screen, or in your device's notification settings for the App.

5. Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services, each with its own privacy policy:

  • Google Play Services - Privacy Policy
  • Google AdMob - About AdMob & data
  • Google Analytics for Firebase - Privacy & Security

6. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided us with information, please contact us so we can remove it.

7. Data Sharing & Security

We do not sell your personal data. Information processed by the App is limited to what the advertising, analytics and platform services listed above require to function. We take reasonable measures to protect any data handled by the App.

8. Your Choices

  • Reset or opt out of ad personalization via your device's privacy/ads settings.
  • Turn off reminder notifications in the App's settings or in your device settings.
  • Uninstall the App at any time to stop all further data processing by it.

9. Data Retention and Deletion

Game progress and settings are stored locally on your device and are removed when you uninstall the App. We do not maintain user accounts. Data processed by advertising and analytics services is retained according to those providers' policies. To request deletion of any data associated with you, contact us at the email address below and we will respond within a reasonable time.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
